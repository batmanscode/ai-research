# Decision theory

- **[Who gets the Infinity Stones?](infinity-stones/)** — a proved, conditional
  robustness result for repeated risky disarmament. The mean time until
  production returns cannot, by itself, certify any positive handover risk
  under positive exponential discounting. A bounded return-time interval gives
  a sharp safety threshold. This is a research note and interactive model,
  not an empirical policy recommendation or a claim of established priority.
