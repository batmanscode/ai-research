# Who gets the Infinity Stones? — design contract

## Reader promise

No prior maths vocabulary should be needed. Show useful equations beside
plain-language explanations: the same-average arithmetic and the decision
rule are visible. Keep the time-weight definition and secondary settings
optional; readers can follow the story without expanding them.

A curious reader should leave understanding why the same 20-year average can
produce opposite handover decisions, and what extra information restores a
guarantee. Lead with the question, make the counterexample interactive, then
explain the proved boundary and the model's limits.

## Visual language

Use the repository's warm paper and dark ink, forest for safety within the
model, violet for concentrated power, and restrained amber for uncertainty.
Faceted stones are inline SVG geometry, not branded Marvel artwork. The SG
home link and the existing favicon supply family identity. No hero mascot,
stock imagery, remote fonts, autoplay, or decorative scientific chart.

The header follows the shared research navigation: Explainer, Proof ↗,
Log ↗, and GitHub ↗. The hero's experiment link starts the interactive story.

## Narrative and interaction

1. Question and direct scenario credit to CYBRDELIC, with a clear research-note
   status. Six geometric stones lead into the concentration bottleneck.
2. Explain the recurring loop: keep; hand over; fail or gain a pause; factories
   return. Distinguish model assumptions from facts. Before the experiments,
   a visible box explains that permanent prevention needs only one risky
   collection, links its calculation, and identifies surviving production as
   the experiments' assumption.
3. Same-average comparison. Three explicitly described return laws, a marker
   for chosen handover risk, exact thresholds, and expected discounted safe
   time. Changes update every value and accessible text together.
4. Controls use range inputs with labels, numerical outputs, and visible units.
   A discount of zero is an intentional, supported special case, not a hidden
   division-by-zero. Reset restores the reproducible counterexample.
5. A mean/support explorer shows a **range of allowable thresholds**, not a
   probability distribution, confidence interval, or statistical error bar.
   Its floor and ceiling are explicit guaranteed limits. Mean stays fixed.
6. The stronger mean-only limit and the patience/uncertainty distinction follow
   the concrete example. Formal proof, references, methods, and caveats remain
   readable without JavaScript; an honest noscript message explains controls.

## Acceptance

No horizontal overflow at 1440, 1024, 390, or 320 px. All range inputs and
buttons support keyboard use, focus is visible, controls have 44 px touch
height, charts use text as well as color, and chart dimensions/labels stay
consistent with values. SVGs have accessible titles/descriptions. No timed
animation. Reduced-motion preference suppresses CSS transitions.

Gemini reviews screenshots for legibility/hierarchy only, after direct
inspection. Mathematical correctness is established separately. The page must
not call itself an empirical forecast, a policy prescription, a novel general
probability theorem, or a solution of the original three-way strategic game.
